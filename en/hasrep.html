VBT system

Velocity-based Training,From Device To App.

Velocity-based training (VBT) manages bar speed, not just load. Has Rep reads that speed from a compact bar-mounted sensor; the app turns data into training.

Movement velocity

Reads the concentric velocity of every rep. Slower bar at the same load means fatigue; faster means you’re fresh.

Automatic rep counting

The device segments and counts reps itself. Half reps and re-racks aren’t counted; your phone can stay in your pocket.

Spatial form analysis

Tracks bar path and movement quality in 3D; flags drifting reps and keeps the raw trace for the coach.

AI: build & analyse

The app builds a programme from your data, analyses every workout and reports progress.

Signature feature

Start the workout,
put the phone away.

You never touch the phone during training. The pod logs every rep to its own memory; nothing is lost even if the connection drops. When you finish, it syncs and the app asks about the few things it isn’t sure of, card by card — swipe right to confirm, left to fix. Thirty seconds, done.

FAQ

Frequently Asked.

Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp or hasrep@canhastech.com.

What exactly does Has Rep measure?
Mean concentric bar velocity (m/s), in-set velocity loss (%) and rep count are the core metrics. Peak velocity, power (W), velocity zones and estimated 1RM are added on top. On the form side it shows bar tilt, rep consistency and stability.
Do I need my phone with me during training?
No. The pod logs everything to its own memory; the phone can stay in your pocket, locker or at home. You sync after the workout. If the phone is nearby you also get a live preview.
How reliable is rep counting? Does it count ghost reps?
Movements like unracking and re-racking are filtered out; reps the app isn’t sure about are asked via swipe-to-confirm after the workout. Every logged rep is therefore verified.
Which exercises does it work with?
Anything with a bar: squat, bench press, deadlift, row, overhead press and Olympic lifts. The magnetic clip attaches in seconds. The first release supports the main lifts with the highest accuracy; the list keeps growing.
Does form analysis give a millimetre-accurate bar path like a camera?
No — honestly, motion sensors can’t produce a millimetre-accurate bar path. Instead Has Rep reliably measures horizontal drift as zones (green/amber/red), rep consistency and wobble/stability.
Which platforms is the app on?
The first release is for iOS. Android is on the roadmap; join the waiting list via the demo form.
What about battery and charging?
The pod sleeps when idle and wakes when shaken. The LED is red while charging and green when full. All LED states are explained in the user manual section.
Can I track multiple athletes as a coach?
Yes — multi-device setup and a coach view are planned for club and coach use. Select “Club / coach” in the demo request and we’ll talk separately.
