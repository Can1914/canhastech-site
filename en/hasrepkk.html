Quick start

Ready in four steps.

Fully charge the device before first use (until the LED is green). The rest takes a minute.

1
Charge itPlug in via USB-C. The LED is red while charging and green when full.
2
Install the appInstall Has Rep from the App Store and sign up with your email.
3
Shake and pairShake the device gently; tap the pod icon in the top right of the app and select your device. The LED turns blue.
4
Clip on the bar, goSeat it on the bar with the magnetic clip, tap “Start Workout”, put the phone away. When done, sync and confirm your reps.

What does the LED mean?

The single LED on top shows three states. Click a row to see it on the device.

02 — Waking the deviceShake

Shake it, it wakes.

When idle, Has Rep goes to sleep to save battery. No button needed to wake it: just shake the device gently . The LED blinks once and the device is ready to connect.

Having trouble?

Try these first.

Still stuck? Write to: hasrep@canhastech.com — or use the WhatsApp button bottom right.

The device doesn’t show up in the app
Shake the device to wake it (the LED blinks once). Make sure Bluetooth is on. Tap the pod icon in the top right and scan again. If it still doesn’t appear, charge it for a minute and retry.
The LED never lights up
The battery may be fully drained; charge for at least 30 minutes. If the red LED doesn’t come on while charging, try another cable and adapter.
Reps were under- or over-counted
You can fix doubtful reps on the swipe-to-confirm screen after the workout. Make sure the device sits firmly on the bar and avoid shaking the bar between sets.
No data after sync
Restart the sync with the device close to the phone (1–2 m). Fully closing and reopening the app usually helps. Data stays in the device memory and isn’t lost.
How do I clean the device?
Wipe with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Don’t submerge, don’t use solvents. Keep the magnetic clip away from metal shavings.
